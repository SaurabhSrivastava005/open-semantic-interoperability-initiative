# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive and evidence-led community. Participants must be able to question assumptions, report negative findings and disagree with proposed designs without personal hostility.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Respect confidentiality, privacy and intellectual-property boundaries.
- Welcome domain knowledge and implementation evidence from different industries and roles.
- Correct errors openly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation, unauthorised disclosure and attempts to suppress legitimate findings are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and reviewed by more than one maintainer where practical.
