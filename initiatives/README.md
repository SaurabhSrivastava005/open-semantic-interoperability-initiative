# SIEL Initiatives

Initiatives apply the SIEL method to bounded, measurable industry problems.

| Initiative | Industry | Status | Initial evidence target |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Healthcare Semantic Fidelity Benchmark](healthcare-semantic-fidelity-benchmark/README.md) | Healthcare and life sciences | Proposed | H3 synthetic cross-platform evidence |

An initiative must define its problem, non-duplication boundary, authoritative standards, safety constraints, test fixtures, evidence measures, governance and limitations before implementation claims are published.
