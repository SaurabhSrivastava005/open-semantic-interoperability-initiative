## 2. Operational problem

## 3. Baseline

## 4. Participants

Identify producers, consumers, accountable owners and reviewers.

## 5. Minimum semantic scope

List only the concepts, relationships and attributes required by this use case.

## 6. Authoritative sources

Identify the authority for every material concept and rule.

## 7. Contract

Define schema, events or API behaviour, validation rules, quality expectations and service conditions.

## 8. Policy and permitted use

Record privacy, security, regulatory, retention and purpose limitations.

## 9. Source mappings

Record exact, conditional, broader, narrower, derived, transformed, unresolved and prohibited mappings.

## 10. Conformance tests

Define fixtures, expected results and failure conditions.

## 11. Measures

Define time to value, effort, accuracy, reuse, change resilience, governance overhead and business outcome.

## 12. Limitations and open questions

Record ambiguity, disagreement and evidence gaps.
