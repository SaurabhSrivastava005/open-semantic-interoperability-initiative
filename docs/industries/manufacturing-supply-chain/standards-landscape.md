# Manufacturing, Automotive and Supply Chain Standards Landscape

## Scope

This landscape covers industrial assets, product lifecycle, operational exchange, digital twins, automotive data spaces, trade and supply-chain events.

## Standards and models

| Standard or framework | Primary purpose | Availability and boundary | SIEL treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Asset Administration Shell](https://industrialdigitaltwin.org/en/content-hub/aasspecifications) | Standard digital representation of industrial assets | Public specifications and open implementations | Use for asset and digital-twin profiles |
| [OPC UA](https://opcfoundation.org/about/opc-technologies/opc-ua/) | Industrial communication and companion information models | Specifications and certification under foundation terms | Profile companion models and test implementations |
| [Catena-X](https://catena-x.net/) | Governed automotive data ecosystem | Open standards, semantic models and use-case standards | Reuse governed industry patterns and test portability |
| [Eclipse Tractus-X](https://projects.eclipse.org/projects/automotive.tractusx) | Open-source Catena-X implementation foundation | Open components and reference implementations | Use for executable tests where suitable |
| [GS1 EPCIS and CBV](https://www.gs1.org/standards/epcis) | Supply-chain visibility events and vocabularies | Public standards under GS1 governance | Use for traceability event profiles |
| [UN/CEFACT](https://unece.org/trade/uncefact) | Trade facilitation and semantic standards | Public recommendations, models and artifacts | Map cross-border trade documents and concepts |
| [ISO 10303 STEP](https://www.iso.org/standard/63141.html) | Product data representation and exchange | ISO-controlled standards and licensing | Reference official versions and avoid unauthorised redistribution |
| [Eclipse ESMF and SAMM](https://projects.eclipse.org/projects/dt.esmf) | Semantic aspect models for digital twins | Open-source tooling and modelling language | Evaluate as a profile authoring and generation foundation |

## Priority SIEL profiles

1. Product and component traceability.
2. Digital product passport exchange.
3. Asset maintenance and condition data.
4. Supplier quality-event exchange.
5. Automotive data-space interoperability.

Profiles must preserve product identity, batch or serial context, event time, custody, provenance and usage-policy obligations.
